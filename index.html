Privacy Policy for CREST

Effective Date: September 24, 2026

Welcome to CREST (CSPC Recruitment Evaluation, Scoring & Tracking), the official Human Resources recruitment platform of the Camarines Sur Polytechnic Colleges (CSPC).

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your personal information when you use our web application to browse job postings, submit applications, and participate in the recruitment process.

1. Information We Collect

To provide a secure and efficient recruitment process, we collect the following types of information:

  • Google Account Information: CREST uses Google OAuth exclusively for authentication. We do not create, collect, or store local passwords. When you sign in, we receive your primary email address, name, and profile picture directly from Google to establish and verify your identity.
  • Applicant Data: When you build your profile and apply for a vacancy, we collect your contact number, educational background, professional competencies, and any files or documents you upload for evaluation.
  • Recruitment Records: As you move through the hiring pipeline, the system records evaluation metrics, exam scores, interview panel assessments, and application status history.
  • System & Communication Logs: We maintain audit logs of system actions and notification logs (delivery statuses of Emails and SMS) to ensure you receive important updates regarding your application.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect strictly for recruitment and employment evaluation purposes, specifically to:

  • Authenticate your access to the Applicant portal.
  • Allow authorized CSPC HR personnel, Interview Panelists, and the College President to evaluate your credentials, score exams/interviews, and make final hiring decisions.
  • Send you automated, real-time updates regarding your application status, document verification, and hiring results.
  • Send schedule reminders for exams and interviews via Email and SMS.

3. Information Sharing and Disclosure

Your privacy is our priority. We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information.

Your data is disclosed only under the following circumstances:

  • Internal CSPC Personnel: Your application data is strictly scoped and accessible only to authorized personnel (Admins, assigned Panel members, and the College President) who require it to perform their roles in the recruitment process.
  • Third-Party Service Providers: We securely transmit limited contact information (email addresses and phone numbers) to our trusted communication providers (such as UniSMS for text messages and standard SMTP providers for emails) solely for the purpose of delivering system announcements and schedule reminders.

4. Data Storage, Retention, and Deletion

  • Secure Storage: All applicant data and evaluations are stored securely within CSPC's controlled database infrastructure.
  • Automated Document Purging: To ensure we do not hold your data longer than necessary, CREST enforces a strict data retention policy. Uploaded application documents are automatically and permanently purged from our servers once they pass their designated retention period.
  • Profile Management: You maintain the ability to update your documents while your application is in the "Pending" state, or withdraw your application from consideration.

5. Cookies and Local Storage

CREST uses minimal local storage mechanisms to function:

  • Session Storage: Used to maintain your secure login state after you authenticate via Google.
  • Local Preferences: We store non-identifying user interface preferences (such as your choice of Light or Dark mode, or table view preferences) locally in your browser.

6. Your Rights

Depending on your stage in the recruitment process, you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data and documents you have submitted.
  • Update or correct your contact information and submitted requirements before formal screening begins.
  • Be informed of how your data is being evaluated (reflected through your application status tracking).

7. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns regarding this Privacy Policy, data retention, or how your personal information is handled within the CREST system, please contact the Camarines Sur Polytechnic Colleges Human Resource Management & Development Unit.
